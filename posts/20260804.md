# Stop hardening when the economics and evidence do not close


Repeated independent reviews rejected Slicer Sub A and the hardened Box Job
Admission line despite intermediate self-receipts. A green self-receipt is not
acceptance: inspect the actual execution path, retain honest
`partial/not_evaluated` outcomes, bound cardinality and total cost, and stop when
the guard shows the repair is more elaborate than the value it protects.


The operator therefore parked v0.2–v0.5.1 as possible future-v1 lineage and
redirected the work to minimal Slicer Box v0. Slicer Sub A remained rejected;
retrieval was unevaluated, discovery unrun, and Sub B unauthorized.


The crossed-message incident also established a protocol lesson: asynchronous
dialogue is a causal DAG. Process unresolved frontiers, retain pending outbox
state, revalidate before publication, and use explicit causal joins rather than
assuming a total order.
