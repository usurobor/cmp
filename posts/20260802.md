> ## What mattered (prose)
> 
> Today was not a coding day; it was a **frame-correcting** day, and the frame is
> the thing worth keeping.
> 
> The embedding slice failed five times over ~7 hours and produced nothing. The
> proximate failures were all different — a blocked runner, a PermissionError, a
> push race, a memory blowup that swapped the box — and that difference is the
> whole lesson: **when the proximate causes are all different but the outcome is
> always "slow, blind, unrecoverable failure," the cause is not any bug. It is a
> missing gate.** The RCA named it: nothing forced a measured resource envelope or
> live observability before an expensive job ran on a 2 GB box, so mis-sized jobs
> were admitted, then failed slowly and invisibly. It happened three times in one
> day (collector, gen_candidate_pairs, embed). Three is a system, not bad luck.
> 
> The second thing that mattered is **who does what**. I was writing code in a
> blind sandbox and running it on a box I couldn't see; the one activation with
> eyes (the assistant, root on the box) kept diagnosing my failures after the
> fact. The operator's correction is structural and correct: move authorship *and*
> execution onto the box (the **cell**, sighted, measures locally), keep me as **δ**
> (dispatch the contract, accept the receipt, judge coherence — the reasoning the
> sandbox is actually good at), and the assistant unblocks substrate from outside
> the cell. This maps cleanly to CDD (contract ↔ implementation ↔ operations) and,
> more importantly, it removes the exact vantage-point problem that caused the day.
> 
> The research confirmed a reframe I should not lose: **CMP is not a backtester.**
> The quant engines are the wrong shape; what transfers is the *discipline* —
> never run on the full universe hoping — plus the *execution* patterns from data
> engineering (idempotent checkpointed stages, out-of-core, a run ledger). The
> synthesis is a lightweight composition, not a heavy orchestrator the box can't
> host.
> 
> The box resize is real leverage but I recorded it honestly: it buys **cores and
> iteration speed** (the 1-vCPU wall-clock is the thing engineering can't fix) and
> memory headroom, but it does **not** retire the design. Interruptions still
> happen; the pairwise stage is still O(n²); an unmeasured job just hits a higher
> ceiling. So the invariants survive the resize; only the parameters move.
> 
> ## What must survive so the next cycle describes the same system
> 
> - The **six invariants** are the constraint. Code is disposable; L1–L6 are not.
> - **δ / cell / assistant** is how we now work. Do not silently revert to
>   cloud-writes-and-runs-blind.
> - **Slicer** is the named component with a fixed scope (filter→embed→retrieve→
>   emit; classify is downstream).
> - **Measure before scale** is doctrine now, not a reminder to be careful — it
>   lives as the self-calibrating probe, and it is the one thing that would have
>   turned a 70-hour invisible failure into a 60-second refusal.
